ourservices

  • Upholsterycleaning

    Sofas, armchairs, chairs and mattresses. We remove stains and odors, then dry the furniture with professional air movers.

    To get a price: just send a photo of the furniture you'd like cleaned.

  • Air conditionerscleaning

    We clean dust, dirt and mold out of the indoor unit so your AC blows clean air again.

    To get a price: send a photo showing where the unit is mounted.

  • Moldremoval

    We find mold and at-risk areas with a thermal camera, an endoscope and a pin moisture meter for walls. Then we run an ozone treatment, which kills the mycelium and airborne spores. After that we remove the mold from surfaces with professional products.

    To get a price: we estimate it from a photo or on site.

  • Ozone treatmentfrom A to Z

    Powerful ozone disinfection of the whole space. It kills mold, bacteria and viruses and leaves the air clean, fresh and safe. The treatment takes 2–6 hours, and afterwards we check the ozone level with a meter.

    To get a price: tell us the floor area of your apartment or house.

    How to prepare your apartment
    • move plants to the balcony or the stairwell;
    • take your pets out;
    • stay out of the apartment during the treatment;
    • give us the Wi-Fi name and password so the device can run remotely.

  • Cleaningkitchens & bathrooms

    We clean grease, limescale and dirt off stoves, kitchen cabinets, bathroom fixtures and floors.

    To get a price: send photos and the floor area.
